ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Revenue for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2025 was $94.9 billion, an increase of 8% compared to the prior year. The increase was driven primarily by higher demand for cloud services and AI-related products, partially offset by a decline in legacy hardware sales.

Gross margin improved to 45.2% from 43.1% in the prior year, reflecting a favorable shift toward higher-margin software and services revenue, as well as ongoing cost discipline in manufacturing.

Operating expenses totaled $21.3 billion, an increase of 5% year-over-year. Research and development expenses grew 12% to $8.7 billion, reflecting increased investment in artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities. Selling, general and administrative expenses declined 2% due to workforce optimization initiatives.

Net income attributable to common shareholders was $23.1 billion, or $5.67 per diluted share, compared to $20.8 billion, or $5.04 per diluted share, in the prior year.

ITEM 7A. QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE DISCLOSURES ABOUT MARKET RISK

The company is exposed to foreign currency risk, interest rate risk, and commodity price risk. Foreign currency exposures arise from transactions denominated in currencies other than the US dollar, primarily the Euro, British Pound, and Japanese Yen. The company uses derivative instruments, including forward contracts and options, to manage these exposures.

As of September 30, 2025, the notional amount of outstanding foreign currency derivative contracts was $12.4 billion. A hypothetical 10% adverse change in foreign currency exchange rates would result in a decrease in the fair value of these contracts of approximately $1.2 billion.

ITEM 8. FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AND SUPPLEMENTARY DATA

The consolidated balance sheet as of September 30, 2025 reports total assets of $178.3 billion, total liabilities of $112.7 billion, and total shareholders equity of $65.6 billion. Cash and cash equivalents were $14.2 billion, and short-term investments were $22.8 billion, providing total liquidity of $37.0 billion.

Accounts receivable, net of allowances, were $18.9 billion. Inventory was $4.1 billion, a decrease of 15% from the prior year due to improved supply chain management. Goodwill and intangible assets totaled $48.2 billion, reflecting acquisitions completed during the fiscal year.

Long-term debt was $42.5 billion, with a weighted average interest rate of 3.8%. The company repurchased $15.0 billion of common stock during the fiscal year under its share repurchase program.
